# 🛡️ BrandShield AI – AI-Powered Brand Governance and Compliance Platform

## 📌 About the Project

BrandShield AI is an AI-powered brand governance and compliance platform designed to help organizations maintain consistent brand identity across their digital content.

It analyses content against approved brand guidelines, terminology, messaging and brand facts. It helps identify inconsistencies, unsupported claims and brand guideline violations while providing suggestions for improvement.

## 🎯 Problem Statement

Organizations create content across multiple platforms and teams. Maintaining consistent brand messaging, tone, terminology and approved information can be challenging.

Manual brand reviews take time and may result in inconsistencies or unsupported claims.

BrandShield AI aims to simplify this process through AI-powered content analysis and brand compliance checking.

## 💡 Proposed Solution

BrandShield AI provides a centralized platform where users can manage brand guidelines and analyse content using AI.

The system compares submitted content with brand-specific information and generates compliance feedback to support better brand governance.

## ✨ Key Features

- User registration and login.
- Brand profile management.
- Brand guidelines and approved terminology management.
- Document upload and content extraction.
- AI-powered brand compliance analysis.
- Identification of tone and terminology inconsistencies.
- Detection of unsupported claims.
- Compliance scoring and feedback.
- Compliance report generation.
